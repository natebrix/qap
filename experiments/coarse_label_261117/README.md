# Frozen-node coarse-label CP gate

This experiment adds one fixed pool of local multi-label moment inequalities to the regional CP projection. The layer jointly updates coarse and regional multipliers. The existing certified DNN bound uses the sum of their symmetric lifted objective terms, the corresponding RHS deduction and their magnitude allowances.

For a fixed node, the free positions on the region side are partitioned into disjoint labels. Each inequality sees up to six selected elements on the other side. Its coefficient numerators use denominator 1024. The solver enumerates the capacity-feasible local label configurations and derives the RHS in integer arithmetic. In the lifted normal, a second-moment coefficient is divided between the two symmetric off-diagonal positions. The pool is specific to the selected node and has a maximum of 50 rows. Each half-space uses sparse entries and a precomputed overlap with other coarse rows and regional moment statistics.

The separator uses 5:8 grid quadrants on tho40t and four average-linkage clusters of the first matrix on tai60b. It builds subsets from heavy flow edges and random seeds. It projects the local moment vector onto the affine hull of the configurations, separates it by LP, removes the coefficient's affine-null component, rounds the result, and reevaluates the direction against the original iterate. The ranking uses the smaller of the original and affine-repaired violations, divided by the lifted Frobenius norm.

## Comparison protocol

The driver loads the selected recipe's numerical DNN settings, including `dnn.beta_sched = w=0.36,wc=2.88`. On each fixed reduced QAP it runs an ordinary cold seed and a regional CP phase. A checkpoint contains the final primal and dual matrices, the entire regional pool in cut order including zero multipliers, and the best certified seed bound. Use checkpoints generated by the driver from the same instance and fixes. The replay treats the stored seed bound as a trusted input. The bounds printed by the continuation solver are independently certified.

Both continuations restart the ADMM iteration and eigensolver schedule from that same checkpoint and the same regional multipliers. They refresh regional triangles every 100 iterations. The cut arm additionally loads the fixed coarse pool at zero multipliers. The scripts compare certified running best bounds at equal iterations and at the regional arm's CPU budget, charging the separator and coarse projector's setup to the cut arm. The seed is shared, so its CPU is outside the comparison budget.

The gate models a frozen same-node continuation. It uses the production numerical recipe and region sets, with a cold first solve and explicit phase lengths. It omits production branch-and-bound inheritance and context-dependent phase stopping. The gate measures finite-budget certificate trajectories. The pool has no child substitution, and the setup makes no claim about the converged relaxation optimum.

The tho40t tests show small gains at equal iterations and weaker certificates at equal CPU. None reached the cutoff. Several additional directions were active, so the weak outcome was not caused by a disabled pool. At iteration 500, the two 40-cut experiments had 37 and 34 active coarse multipliers. Regional P-steps took about 3.5–4.7 ms and the coarse variants about 12.8–14.1 ms. D5's 40-cut pool included four cut directions with numerical PSD witnesses against all union binary moment hulls, while 25 were already implied by those hulls and the affine equations in the local LP model.

The higher-order pool used 23 distinct subsets from 12 flow seeds and 12 random draws. Each subset had 4,096 configurations. There were 20 positive candidates. The union LP excluded eight, leaving 12 tested directions, of which 10 were active at iteration 500. The local PSD mixing check supplied no positive witness for this pool. The higher-order comparison reused the d5 regional baseline from the same checkpoint and executable. Its ranking, local control and projector setup consumed about 10.49 CPU s.

The three tho40t comparisons do not support a larger subtree gate. They test selected fixed pools at finite budgets and leave the full optimal objective impact of this cut family unresolved.

The tai60b test shared a 585,899,572.815 seed certificate after 100 ordinary and 200 regional CP iterations. Both fixed first-side indices exhausted the original two-element cluster. The four original cells therefore had residual capacities [14, 0, 8, 36], with 81 and 243 local configurations on four and five elements. The regional pool began with 3,000 cuts over 69 mapped regions. The final coarse pool had 32 active multipliers. Its P-step averaged 71.4 ms versus 21.6 ms, and the separator plus projector setup cost 3.04 CPU s. The candidate reached the regional arm's final certificate at 202.81 check CPU seconds versus 188.80. Earlier shared thresholds also took more checked CPU. The +470,059 gain at 120 iterations leaves about 12.94 million to the cutoff. This test is at an early certificate and does not settle the potential near the production bound.

The solver's `make test` passed, including all 7 reference cases with exact node counts. The optional AWS coordinator scenario skipped because boto3 and moto were unavailable. Brute-force seeds 2 and 3 each passed 4,536 checks. The separate coarse validation exhausted small asymmetric QAPs in both region orientations, including four reduced-node cases and signed dyadic RHS values. Eight active cases produced 32 finite CP/quiet certificates and 80 lazy checks. The maximum printed certificate minus the exact optimum was −1.0169e−6. The follow-up validation included order-six random coefficients and four labels. Eight active cases produced 33 finite certificates and 80 lazy checks, with no printed bound above the optimum.

## Reproduction

Use a single BLAS thread. This Linux build linked the SciPy-supplied OpenBLAS 32-bit interface. Set `blas_dir` to the directory that contains the library on the current machine.

```sh
make -j2 -C latest dnncuts \
  DNN_LAPACK="-L$blas_dir -l:libscipy_openblas-6cdc3b4a.so -Wl,-rpath,$blas_dir" \
  DNN_LAPACK_DEFS='-DDNN_BLAS_PREFIX=scipy_ -DDNN_OPENBLAS'

export OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1
python3 experiments/coarse_label_261117/verify.py
python3 experiments/coarse_label_261117/run_gate.py /tmp/coarse-gate tho40_d5 tho40_d3 tai60b_d2
python3 experiments/coarse_label_261117/local_control.py \
  /tmp/coarse-gate/tho40_d5/coarse.json /tmp/coarse-gate/tho40_d5/local-controls.json

mkdir -p /tmp/coarse-t6/tho40_d5
ln -s /tmp/coarse-gate/tho40_d5/seed.cp /tmp/coarse-t6/tho40_d5/seed.cp
ln -s /tmp/coarse-gate/tho40_d5/seed.Y.bin /tmp/coarse-t6/tho40_d5/seed.Y.bin
ln -s /tmp/coarse-gate/tho40_d5/regional.log /tmp/coarse-t6/tho40_d5/regional.log
python3 experiments/coarse_label_261117/run_gate.py /tmp/coarse-t6 tho40_d5 \
  --reuse-seed --reuse-regional --sizes 6 --samples 12 --cut-count 20 --binary-filter
```

`local_control.py` solves an outer LP with the exact local binary moment hull for every union of coarse labels and the local configuration affine constraints. It can filter LP-redundant directions and test numerical PSD witnesses by convex mixing. A positive LP violation alone leaves PSD nonredundancy open. This small local model does not establish global QAP bound improvement.
